## Key findings from the sweep (plain language)

1. Tagore's 1905 anti-partition record is real and multi-form: a co-signed unity proclamation in his own magazine, rakhi mobilisation on partition-day itself, and multiple protest songs — all in the window of the partition crisis.
2. The DU-opposition story is real — but the documented opponents are the Calcutta University circle (Ashutosh Mukherjee, Surendranath Banerjea, Rasbihari Ghosh's camp) and ~200 East Bengal Hindus memorialising against it ("Mecca University" taunt in Freedom Movement vol). **The one claim that Tagore presided over the Garer Math protest rests on M.A. Matin (2000) without citation; Tagore's own dated letters + a cash-book voucher place him at Shilaidaha that entire fortnight.**
3. Tagore's East Bengal record is administrative + welfare (estates at Shilaidaha/Shahzadpur/Patisar; an agricultural bank at ~12% p.a. with ~Rs 90,000 deployed per the 1916 Gazetteer; Hitabadi Sabha 1905), which later became the Visva-Bharati Central Co-operative Bank (1927).
4. The anthem's path: 1905 protest song → 1971 war anthem → 1972 Order + Constitution Article 4(1). Charter LC9 needs the Article 4 correction.
5. Honest counter-material exists: Tagore himself recorded that Muslims largely did not join (and partly opposed) the Swadeshi movement (Rachanabali vol 24, p 33); most Bengali Muslims saw him as the "other" through Partition (Frontline/Murshid); his wealth came partly from East Bengal zamindari. None of this equals "against Bangladesh," but the script must not hide it.
6. **Addendum sweep (agents D/E/F):** the religious critique of ASB is real and documented but is **theological interpretation + political mobilisation**, not a textual feature — no polytheistic line exists in the song's text; objections date from 1975+, while the 1972 founding record shows none. The "foreign poet" objection has real political patrons (1979, 2002, 2024–25) but world precedent runs the other way (Imber, Sontonga, Pottier). Tagore's household did practise child marriage (Mrinalini 1883, Renuka 1901) and his zamindari drew named Muslim critics — all documented; the 1906 relief was led by Nivedita/Ashwini Dutta, not him. Angle anchor: **respect for the literary contribution, with the man's record shown warts-and-all.**
