Bridge = let a local server feed jobs: extension polls GET /api/through/pending?claim=1 every minute and POSTs results back. Any server speaking the contract works — studio :4000 is just the default URL.

Site packs = declarative JSON: match domains + named selectors + preset actions. Add a new site without touching the engine. Bundled: generic, flow, aistudio, youtube, facebook, tiktok.

Flows = reusable job templates (data, not code). Video ref→scene→clip is just one example flow here — the engine also runs research, form-fill, upload, QA flows.

Tips & Tricks
Use double line breaks to separate prompts in your text file.
Set human-like delays to reduce bot detection risk.
Use "Veo 3.1 Quality" for important videos, "Fast" for bulk generation.
Enable auto-download and disable "Ask where to save" in Chrome settings.
