## Key Synthesis of the Sweep (Plain Language)
1. **The Myth of Sudden Barbaric Eradication:** The Bronze Age did not vanish in an afternoon because of one invading armada. It was a prolonged, multi-decadal cascading systems failure (~1200–1150 BCE).
2. **The Fragility of Hyper-Specialized Globalization:** Just like modern semiconductor supply chains depend on rare earth metals, the Bronze Age military-industrial complex depended on tin mined thousands of kilometers away (e.g. Badakhshan/Tajikistan). Severing sea routes paralyzed armies.
3. **The Multiplier Effect:** Paleoclimatologists have definitively proven a severe, multi-decade megadrought. The Sea Peoples were not just bloodthirsty pirates; they traveled in family ox-carts with children and livestock — they were climate refugees turned desperate raiders.
4. **Permanent vs. Resilient Outcomes:** Decentralized, literate cultures adapted (Phoenicians, early Israelites), while rigidly centralized palatial bureaucracies (Mycenae, Hatti) experienced total system death.
