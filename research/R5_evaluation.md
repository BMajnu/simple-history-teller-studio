# R5 — SOURCE EVALUATION (CARS Framework)

Every source identified in R4 is scored on the four CARS criteria (**C**redibility, **A**ccuracy, **R**easonableness, **S**upport) and assigned one of four operational stamps:
- **`[VERIFIED]`**: Screen-usable as objective historical fact. Primary documents or top-tier peer-reviewed consensus.
- **`[ATTRIBUTED]`**: Usable only with explicit on-screen attribution ("Historian X argues...", "Egyptian temple records claim...").
- **`[OPINION]`**: Scholarly interpretation or disputed theory; presented within an opinion or hypothesis frame.
- **`[CIRCULATING]`**: Popular cultural myth, debunked claim, or unverified narrative. Must be addressed explicitly in an allegation/debunking frame.

## Circulating & Debunked Narratives Log (Mandatory QA Guardrails)

* **`[CIRCULATING-01]` The "Baking in the Kiln" Romantic Legend:**
  * *Claim:* Scribes at Ugarit were baking tablet RS 20.238 in an oven at the precise moment sea raiders smashed through the door.
  * *Source:* Claude Schaeffer (1950s initial report).
  * *Fact-check status:* **DEBUNKED by Itamar Singer (1999).** The "kiln" was an installation by later post-collapse squatters; the tablets fell from an upper administrative archive.
  * *Editorial mandate:* The video must state: *"জনশ্রুতি আছে চিঠিটি তখন চুল্লিতে পোড়ানো হচ্ছিল, তবে আধুনিক প্রত্নতাত্ত্বিকরা জানান এটি মহাফেজখানা থেকেই উদ্ধার করা হয়েছিল—কিন্তু চিঠির আতঙ্ক ছিল ১০০% বাস্তব।"*
* **`[CIRCULATING-02]` The "Dorian Invasion" Total Conquest:**
  * *Claim:* Barbarian Dorians with iron swords swept down from Central Europe and burned all of Greece.
  * *Fact-check status:* **REJECTED AS MONOCAUSAL.** Archaeological layers show cultural continuity in ceramics (sub-Mycenaean); internal uprising and drought-induced social collapse occurred before any Dorian population movement.
  * *Editorial mandate:* Frame as an outdated 19th-century theory superseded by systems collapse.
* **`[CIRCULATING-03]` The "Sole Sea Peoples Apocalypse":**
  * *Claim:* The Sea Peoples were an invincible horde of barbarian pirates who alone destroyed the civilised world.
  * *Fact-check status:* **REFRAMED AS SYMPTOM.** The Sea Peoples were migrating families (ox-carts, women, children depicted at Medinet Habu) driven by climate catastrophe. They attacked an already fractured, starving world.
