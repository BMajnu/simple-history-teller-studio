## One-sentence message

**In 1905 the man who later became Bangladesh's national anthem's poet stood against the partition that created Dhaka's province — his unity rakhi and his songs are in the record — while the viral accusations against him (opposing Dhaka University, insulting Bangladesh) fail verification in the documents; the religious and "foreign poet" objections to the anthem are real positions on the record but are interpretations and politics, not textual facts or world precedent; and his personal record — child marriages in his own household, the landlord's contested legacy — is neither sainthood nor sabotage, but a paper trail we show as it is: respect for the literature, no blind veneration of the man.**
